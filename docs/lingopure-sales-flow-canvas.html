How it works

Three steps, and the first one is free.

STEP 01

A real class, not a sales call

45 minutes with a LingoPure teacher. Live, in ClassIn. You are assessed while you speak, not by a form.

STEP 02

Your LP‑18 placement report

Within 48 hours: your CEFR level, your micro-level placement across 18 bands, your strengths, and the specific things holding you back.

STEP 03

A programme built on that report

Live classes and micro-lessons targeted at your gaps. Progress re-measured as you go. CEFR-aligned certification through TrackTest.

06 · Proof
